How Is the First Meeting Conducted?

The initial contact is used to understand the general scope of the legal request and arrange a meeting.

  1. 1

    Contact Request

    A brief request is sent through the form or the corporate email address.

  2. 2

    Initial Review

    The general subject is reviewed and information on meeting availability is provided.

  3. 3

    Meeting Arrangement

    An in-person or online meeting is arranged and any documents that may be required are identified.

  4. 4

    Next Steps

    The legal process, fee principles and available courses of action are explained during the meeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I provide for the first meeting?

A general outline of the matter and any important dates or deadlines is sufficient. Please do not send identity documents, medical records or detailed case files in your initial message.

Can the meeting be held online?

Yes. An online meeting may be arranged depending on the nature of the request and the parties' availability.

Do I need to send documents before the meeting?

After the initial review, we will identify any required documents and provide an appropriate secure delivery method.

How is a power of attorney issued?

The scope and method of issuing a power of attorney depend on the particular legal matter and are explained during the meeting.

Can I contact the firm from abroad?

Yes. You may contact the firm through the corporate email address and online meeting channels.

Does submitting the form create an attorney-client relationship?

No. An initial contact request alone does not create an attorney-client or representation relationship; a separate agreement is required.

Data Protection and Privacy Notice

1. Data controller

Büyüknisan Law Firm is the data controller under Turkish Law No. 6698.

2. Data and purpose

If you choose to send an email, your name, email, optional phone number, subject and message are processed to assess your request and contact you.

3. Email transmission

The website does not send form data to a form intermediary. It creates a draft in your device's email application; the message is transmitted through your email provider and the firm's corporate email infrastructure only after you approve sending it.

4. Legal basis and retention

Data is processed for responding to your request, contract-related steps and legitimate interests, and retained only for required legal periods.

5. Your rights

Requests under Art. 11 of Law No. 6698 may be sent to info@buyuknisanhukuk.com.

6. Professional secrecy and secure communication

Information protected under Art. 36 of the Turkish Attorneyship Law is kept as professional secret. Please do not send special-category personal data or confidential documents in your initial message; a secure delivery method will be provided if needed.

7. Essential local storage

The site stores one preference on your device solely to avoid displaying this notice repeatedly. No advertising or visitor analytics cookies are used.
